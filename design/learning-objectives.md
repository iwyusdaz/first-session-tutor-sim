# Learning objectives

Aligned to the five competency domains in [`tutor-training-course/research/competency-model.md`](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/tutor-training-course): Relationships; Session structure; Questioning and errors; Feedback and motivation; Data use. Move names are the canonical names from [`socratic-ai-tutor/spec/pedagogy-rules.md`](https://github.com/iwyusdaz/socratic-ai-tutor).

After a run, the learner can:

| # | Objective | Domain | Where it is practiced |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Run the session routine (warm-up → review → guided practice → independent try → exit ticket) within its time boxes | Session structure | m1–m8 follow the routine; the wall clock and routine bar show the time box |
| 2 | Choose tutoring moves that keep students doing the thinking (probing question, hint ladder before answers) | Questioning and errors | m3, m4, m6, m7 |
| 3 | Notice and respond to cues of confusion, disengagement or dominance | Relationships | Observation notes at every moment; m1, m5 |
| 4 | Use wait time of 3–5 seconds after asking a question | Questioning and errors | m4 and m7 (real-seconds wait, adjustable scale or manual wait) |
| 5 | Respond to errors with a probing question rather than a correction | Questioning and errors | m3 (the error moment follows the mix's misconception) |
| 6 | Use an exit ticket to check each student's understanding | Session structure; Data use | m2 (review from last ticket) and m8 (time check) |

Objective numbers are used in `content/items.json` (pre-check and post-check items).

The objectives describe what the session asks the learner to practice. Whether the sim improves these skills in real sessions has not been tested.
