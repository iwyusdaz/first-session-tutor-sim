# Personas

All personas are fictional design tools, not research participants. No real users were interviewed.

## Tutor personas (learners)

| Persona | Background | Need | What the sim offers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diego, 20, college tutor | Strong in math, new to teaching | Tends to explain instead of asking | Moments where "tell the answer" looks efficient but the replay shows the student's understanding didn't move |
| Grace, 52, career changer | Patient, organized | Misses the quiet student | Run 1 (the quiet student): Talia only speaks if the tutor waits and invites her |
| Ms. Okafor, site coordinator | Coaches a team of tutors | Wants a shared replay to discuss in coaching | Debrief timeline with rubric-domain scores, coach notes and a printable/table view |

## Simulated students (fictional, about age 13)

Constructed for teaching purposes. No real likeness. See `design/character-design.md` for appearance, names and the representation review.

| Student | Name | Pattern | Cues |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Jordan (he) | Confident; answers fast; makes sign errors | Leans in, hand up often, interrupts |
| B | Talia (she) | Quiet; understands more than she shows | Minimal eye contact, stops writing when confused, rarely volunteers |
| C | Sam (they) | Discouraged by earlier failure; "I'm bad at math" | Slumps, arms folded, looks away, short answers |

The patterns are not tied to any demographic trait.
